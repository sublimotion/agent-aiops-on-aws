TLDR

PASS Native MTP (A1) wins: 2.75× goodput at the 40 tok/s floor (4,879 vs 1,771 tok/s at the constrained knee), no crossover through c192, quality gate 3/3, $2.14/M output tokens (−64% vs baseline) [measured]. Decision

  1. Adopt A1 native MTP for interactive (blocking-agent) GLM-5.2 fleets on B200 TP8 — 2.75× knee, no crossover through c192, quality gate 3/3 [measured]. Contradicts the parent B300 trajectory's "MTP −12% @ knee" — that was measured through the synthetic no-chat-template driver (AL 1.6 vs 3.2 here); B300 TP4+DP2 re-measurement with this driver is the reconciliation follow-up.
  2. DFlash: adopt-conditional for c≤64 latency-tier only at draft=16; sweep draft=8 (A2b) before any production use — predicted waste ~1.8, likely competitive.
  3. Avoid DFlash at c≥128 [measured: parity at c128, collapse at c192 — verify batch ~c×17 past compute saturation].
  4. Never gate a thinking model at small max_tokens — Q2 first attempt returned empty at 2048 (finish=length); gate at ≥8192.
  5. Product implication (Drafter Forge): the gap is verify-cost-aware drafter/block-size selection per (model, workload, concurrency) — measure AL on your traffic, compute (k+1)/AL, pick the arm. Acceptance transfer is not the gap (GLM community drafter transferred; K2.7's didn't — both measured).

Caveats & provenance

  • Cache policy: cache-realistic by design (coding-agent card, 92% prefix cacheable) — matches the declared workload realism band; not a cache-inflated run.
  • Synthetic-vs-production: accept-lengths measured on real agentic traffic (metrics-timeline windowed, counter-reset segmented) — not synthetic prompts. The parent's synthetic-driver AL 1.6 is the cautionary counter-example inside this very report.
  • Cross-run confound: B200 TP8 fallback ⇒ arm deltas are within-node and clean; absolute numbers not comparable to B300 TP4+DP2 parent.
  • A2 c1 cell: n=4, aggregate unreliable [measured, low-n]; K2.7 absolute numbers smoke-level (INT4 fallback warnings, no reasoning-parser), the DFlash-vs-A0 delta is real.
