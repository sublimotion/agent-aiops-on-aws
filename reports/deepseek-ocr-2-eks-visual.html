Normalization story · iter 4 single-image vs iter 5 stratified corpus

The req/s collapse. Switching from a single 19 KB image to a 6-document stratified corpus collapsed request throughput 3.7× (25.4 → 6.79 req/s) while output tokens/s held within 18% (4,063 → 3,361). Output tokens/s is the correct capacity metric for OCR; req/s massively undervalues throughput on long-output documents.

Iter 4 vs Iter 5 @ c=32 saturation

Per-doc-type efficiency matrix · batch saturation (c=32)

Counter-intuitive finding. Dense pages deliver 3.3× the output throughput of articles (1,118 vs 323 output tok/s) despite 3.6× longer e2e latency — they max decode-phase utilization by hitting the 1024-token cap. Dense is the cheapest bucket ($2.00/M tokens); handwritten is the most expensive ($13.50/M tokens) because the encoder cost is fixed but the output is minimal.

Concurrency scaling · stratified corpus

Sweep at c = [1, 4, 16, 32], 50 steady-state requests per level. Both request rate and output token/s scale near-linearly through c=16; the c=32 step is partly bounded by the sweep's short window (9.8 s @ c=32). The 60s batch-throughput run pushes the c=32 ceiling to 6.79 req/s & 3,361 output tok/s.

Latency distribution @ c=32 saturation

The tail is bimodal. Short-output buckets (receipt/article/table/handwritten) cluster under 3 s. Formula and dense stretch to ~9.3 s p99 because both hit the 1024-token output cap. Single-pool scheduling will miss a p99 < 3 s SLO whenever long docs batch together.

Known gaps & what's next

In progress

Dataset mirror — olmOCR-bench (1,400 docs, ~800 MB) + OmniDocBench (~1.5 GB) to s3://agent-aiops-bench-us-east-2/datasets/. Unblocks 8 of 11 workload cells.

O3 quality gate — pending dataset; tolerance=0.02 vs DeepSeek's self-reported 76.3 on olmOCR-bench. Harness runners scaffolded (run-olmocr-bench.sh, run-omnidocbench.sh) but exit 2 until mirror completes.

Next iteration

FP8 Pareto row (T1) — expected 1.5-2× throughput lift (3,361 → 5-6K tok/s) and 2× VRAM headroom (max_num_seqs 32 → 64-128).

Wider corpus — real-world dense pages push the image-token ratio to ~16× vs receipts; synthetic corpus is a floor.

Deferred cells — burn-in (O5), cold-start (O9), power-efficiency (O11), and g6e.12xlarge TP=4 scheduled post-O3.
