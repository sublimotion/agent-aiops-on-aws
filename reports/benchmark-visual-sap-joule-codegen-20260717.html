Key Lessons (compounded to steering + cards)

  • Cache-honest rule: never accept a benchmark whose request count exceeds its distinct-prompt count without a prefix-hit-rate audit — it changed this engagement's answer by ~7×.
  • MNBT probe mandatory: untuned chunked-prefill budget cost 20% P90 on a mixed-length workload; probe before any topology verdict.
  • Small-n knees are optimistic: n=48 said 10–12; n=80 said 9. Confirm with ≥8·c samples before quoting concurrency-at-SLA.
  • Spec decode crossover is real: EAGLE3 regresses 41% at the batch operating point on GDDR7; drafters exist but the regime rules them out here.
  • OSL caps mislead: models EOS naturally (~300–800 tok); ask for the measured output-length distribution, not the cap. p99 at cap 2048 hit 116 s.
  • g7e capacity intel: spot 12xl only (64 vCPU quota; raise CASE_OPENED), ODCR dry 36/36 AZ probes, SageMaker ml pool independent — agent account has ml.g7e.48xl quota = 4.
  • NVFP4 is the biggest unmeasured lever: ~3× decode from bytes-read on this architecture, but needs offline requant + SAP sign-off + sm_120 kernel smoke.
