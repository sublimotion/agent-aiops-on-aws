Feature Impact

Measured impact of key optimizations

Prefix Caching

Most impactful single optimization. Qwen3-Next with 8K shared prefix across 1000 concurrent requests:

+77% throughput (40K to 71K tok/s)
-82% TTFT (77s to 13.5s)
Under memory pressure (0.30 GPU util): -94% TTFT

HiCache L2 Offload

GLM-5 on B200 with 100 GB/rank CPU offload:

+71% throughput at 64 concurrent (909 to 1,556)
2.86x peak (909 to 2,602 at 128 conc)
KV cache eviction was the bottleneck, not compute

MTP Speculative Decoding

Results vary dramatically by topology:

GLM-5 on B200 (NVSwitch): Works — 2,374 tok/s
Qwen3-Next on g7e (PCIe): -2% to -41% throughput
NVLink required for speculative verification overhead

Tool Calling (BFCL)

Accuracy across models and engines:

GLM-5 vLLM (glm47 parser): 100% (50/50)
Qwen3-Next vLLM: 91.7% (188/200)
Devstral vLLM: 91.7% (188/200)
SGLang XML format: Partial (non-standard output)

Multi-Replica Scaling

Qwen3-Next 2x TP=4 replicas on p5en:

1.71x throughput scaling (49K to 84K tok/s)
Zero failures at 1,500 concurrent
Near-linear scaling with shared prefix cache

Devstral Reliability

4-replica swarm on g7e (32 concurrent agents):

0% failure rate at all concurrency levels
vs Qwen3-Next SGLang: 11% failures at 32 agents
Dense model = deterministic, no MoE routing variance

Throughput: Self-Hosted Dominates

Our Qwen3-Next at moderate load (2,886 tok/s) is 4.5x faster than the fastest hosted large model (Mercury 2 at 644 tok/s). GLM-5 with HiCache (2,602 tok/s) serves a 744B model faster than any hosted provider serves models 1/10th the size.

Hosted providers are throughput-limited by multi-tenant scheduling. Dedicated GPUs eliminate queueing.

Latency: Hosted Wins at Low Load

Hosted providers have lower cold-start TTFT for simple requests: Gemini Flash-Lite at 0.45s, Mistral Small at 0.54s. Our best TTFT (43ms) is at sustained QPS 4 with warm KV cache — not comparable to single-request cold latency.

At sustained production load, self-hosted latency is competitive or better.

Cost: Depends on Utilization

Self-hosted at $0.46/M tok (g7e Qwen3-Next) beats all hosted except Alibaba's Qwen2.5 Turbo ($0.09) and Gemma 3n ($0.03) — but those are much smaller/weaker models.

For equivalent-capability models: DeepSeek V3 hosted at $0.63 vs our Qwen3-Next at $0.46 — 27% cheaper self-hosted. GPT-4o at $4.38 vs our GLM-5 at $1.12 — 74% cheaper self-hosted.

What Hosted Does Better

Zero ops burden. No AMI management, no DeepGEMM JIT, no NCCL debugging, no capacity blocks. Also: scale to zero (pay nothing when idle), global distribution, and model diversity without hardware changes.

Self-hosted wins on throughput, cost at utilization, and data control. Hosted wins on operational simplicity and burst capacity.
