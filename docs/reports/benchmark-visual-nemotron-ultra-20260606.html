Key Insights

Engine Choice Dominates Hardware — vLLM Beats SGLang on Half the GPUs

The first run (SGLang, B300, TP8) measured single-stream 177 tok/s, accept length 2.4, and concluded 300 tok/s was an inherent model ceiling. That was wrong: SGLang's NemotronH MTP path is broken (upstream bug #21138, ~0% real acceptance). The same weights on vLLM v0.22.0 — running on B200 with TP4, half the GPUs — reach accept length 3.54 and decode 297.7 tok/s, a 1.7× single-stream jump. Native nemotron_h_mtp is the decisive factor.

Node Saturates at ~1,883 tok/s Aggregate at c≥64

vLLM TP4 aggregate throughput plateaus near 1,883 tok/s from c=64 onward (vs ~1,040 on the SGLang run). Accept length holds flat at ~3.56 across the entire sweep (c=1→256) — MTP speculation neither degrades nor improves under concurrency, and there are zero errors throughout.

Production-Stable Under Sustained Saturation

c=256 held for 150s: zero errors, throughput steady at ~1,847 tok/s, no preemption collapse, no KV exhaustion. VRAM ~171/183 GB on the 4 active GPUs at ~248W. Stable and fast.

Long Context Is Fast and Stable to 256K

Decode holds 468–514 tok/s from 64K all the way to ~194K-token prompts — 3.7–4.3× the SGLang figures — with TTFT only 2.4–4.0s and no OOM. The model's max_position_embeddings is 262,144 (256K); the spec's 512K/1M tiers exceed the architectural limit and cannot run.

Retail Price Is the Remaining Gap: ~$6.00/M vs ~$2.50/M (closes with a 2nd replica)

At full batch a single TP4 replica costs ~$6.00 per million output tokens — a managed retail API runs ~$2.50/M, about 2.4× cheaper. But the test used only 4 of 8 GPUs: a projected second TP4 replica on the idle 4 (each replica fits in ~171/183 GB/GPU) roughly doubles aggregate throughput at the same instance cost, dropping $/M to ~$2.99 (ideal 2.0×) or ~$3.33 (realistic 1.8×) — essentially infra-level parity, since our figure carries zero margin while retail includes theirs. Projected, not measured (node torn down before validating replica scaling). A 2nd replica does not change single-stream speed — it serves different requests.

More Acceptance Headroom Likely Exists

Accept length 3.54 was measured at the required temperature=1.0. The MTP head supports stable drafting at longer draft lengths, so lower temperature or draft-length tuning should push acceptance — and therefore single-stream speed — further above the 300 tok/s line.
