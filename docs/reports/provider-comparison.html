Cost: 10% Cheaper than Best Hosted

Our HiCache config at $1.12/M beats DeepInfra ($1.24) by 10%. Without HiCache optimization, our baseline ($3.29) is more expensive than most hosted options — optimization matters more than hardware.

Throughput: 14.6x Faster

Our peak 2,602 tok/s vs Baseten's 177.8 tok/s (fastest hosted). Dedicated GPUs with HiCache L2 offload eliminate multi-tenant queueing entirely. Critical for real-time agent workloads.

Hosted is 15x Cheaper

DeepInfra at $0.12/M vs our $1.88/M. Hosted providers amortize a 24B dense model across thousands of users. A single dedicated GPU can't compete on cost — the GPU sits 95%+ idle between requests at typical coding agent workloads.

When Self-Hosted Still Makes Sense

Data sovereignty, air-gapped environments, or as part of a multi-model fleet where the GPU also serves larger models. Our g7e fleet strategy uses Devstral as overflow capacity on otherwise-idle GPUs — marginal cost approach zero when colocated.

Reasoning Models Don't Self-Host Well (Yet)

Kimi K2.5's extended thinking generates long reasoning chains, making it generation-bound at only 11 tok/s. Hosted providers at $0.28-1.20/M are significantly cheaper. Reasoning models need speculative decoding or hardware acceleration to self-host economically.

First-Party Subsidy

Moonshot AI's $0.28/M is almost certainly subsidized — they're trying to build market share. Expect prices to rise 2-3x once they capture users. Self-hosting is a hedge against API price increases, but only if throughput improves.

Self-Host: Large MoE Models

GLM-5 and Qwen3-Next are clear self-hosting wins. Large MoE models have high per-token cost on hosted providers (many GPUs needed) but sparse activation makes them efficient on dedicated hardware. With optimizations (HiCache, prefix caching), self-hosted beats every hosted provider.

Hosted: Small Dense & Reasoning Models

Devstral at 24B can be served cheaply by hosted providers who batch thousands of users on shared GPUs. A dedicated GPU at $16.57/hr generating 52 tok/s simply can't compete. Kimi K2.5 is generation-bound — hosted providers amortize the cost of long reasoning chains across many users.

The Hybrid Strategy

The optimal approach: self-host large MoE models on B200/g7e (where you have throughput and cost advantage) + use hosted APIs for small dense models and reasoning (where providers have scale advantage). This is the tiered fleet strategy in practice.

The Utilization Trap

Self-hosted cost assumes sustained utilization. At 80% util, GLM-5 costs $1.12/M. At 20% util, it costs $4.48/M — worse than every hosted option. The breakeven is ~55% utilization. Below that, you're paying for idle GPUs.
