Cost-Aware Routing — Phase 1 + 2 Results

9-worker Bedrock pool · ModernBERT classifier-router on 4,067 questions · 2026-05-28 / 29

Bottom line

Five router architectures all fail to beat always-Qwen-Coder-480B.

The +16.8pp per-question oracle gap is real but unreachable by a 149M-class encoder. 8× more training data confirms the limit is signal extraction, not data quantity. Ship always-Qwen-Coder-480B for chatbot traffic; reach for LLM-as-router or RL orchestrator only for agentic workloads.

Diagnosis: signal-extraction limit, not data limit. The classifier collapses to majority class. Best epoch accuracy ≈ Gemma frequency. Closing the +16.8pp oracle gap would require either an LLM-as-classifier with chain-of-thought (defeats the cost purpose) or a richer feature space (response previews, stronger encoder embeddings).

4Two regimes, two router designs

The negative result is specific to single-shot chatbot traffic. It does not generalize to agentic workloads.

Regime A — single-shot chat

  • ~67% solved by Gemma-27B alone at $0.0003/q
  • ~88% covered through Qwen-Coder-480B at ≤$0.0007/q
  • Per-question difficulty isn't extractable from text by 149M encoders
  • Routing overhead (latency + mispick risk) often exceeds the gain
Recommendation: deploy always-Qwen-Coder-480B. Don't train a classifier-router for this traffic mix.

Regime B — agentic / multi-step

  • 20-50 turn trajectories compound per-turn error (90%30 ≈ 4%)
  • Self-critique, backtracking, long-context coherence favor flagships
  • Routing decision is per-turn, not per-question
  • ModernBERT-class encoders cannot model future turns
Recommendation: use an LLM-as-router with chain-of-thought, or an RL-trained orchestrator (Sakana / RL-conductor pattern) over the pool.

Caveat on Regime A. This corpus (MATH500, MMLU-Pro, HumanEval, MBPP, WildChat single-turn) skews easy. A deliberately-hard slice (AIME, GPQA-Diamond, LiveCodeBench-Hard, SWE-bench Lite) would shift the cheapest-correct distribution toward flagship tier and could change the answer. Untested.
