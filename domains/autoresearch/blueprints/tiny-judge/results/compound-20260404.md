## Analysis

This experiment successfully validated that behavioral features from agent trajectories are additive to v009 rubric-based verification, achieving +22.7% AUC improvement. The top features by importance (elapsed_s, total_cost_usd, action_entropy, context_growth_rate) independently confirm the pivot analysis findings from earlier experiments.

The precision ceiling at ~0.81 P@R≥30% confirms a fundamental limitation: diff-only signals plus behavioral telemetry cannot achieve the 0.85 precision target without deeper code understanding. This validates the roadmap toward RLVR or learned verifiers as the next phase.

The excellent calibration (ECE=0.043) means the trained model's predicted probabilities can be used directly as reward signals in downstream RL training without recalibration, enabling the next phase of the research program.

No cross-cutting infrastructure or deployment lessons were found — all findings are specific to the verification ML task and dataset. The two lessons elevated to steering are general Python environment and classical ML modeling patterns that apply to future autoresearch experiments.
