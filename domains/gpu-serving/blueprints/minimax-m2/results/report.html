Headline findings

lever
Prefix caching is the dominant throughput & latency lever. Cold throughput plateaus at ~226 tok/s and TTFT collapses (2.3s→125s by c64). With a shared cacheable prefix, throughput climbs to 1,888 tok/s (TP4) / 2,158 tok/s (TP4+EP4) and TTFT stays low far longer. ~8× aggregate throughput at matched hardware — caching, not offload or parallelism, is the first-order win.
myth-bust
CPU KV offload neither helped nor hurt under reuse. Across every matched point, CPU-offload tracked GPU-only within ±4% (measurement noise), with 0 preemptions. A large offload "regression" is a cold-workload / configuration artifact, not an inherent cost of the offload tier on B200.
bottleneck
The 100K / 90%-reuse regime is KV-capacity-bound. Prefix hit-rate held at 90% as designed, but KV-pool utilization rose with concurrency to 100% at c512, where queue depth jumped to 259 and TTFT hit ~309s. This is the saturation knee: capacity, not prefill compute, caps this regime.
parallelism
Expert parallelism (TP4+EP4) lifts the cached ceiling. On shared-prefix at c128, TP4+EP4 reached 2,158 tok/s vs TP4's 1,544 (+40%) at lower TTFT (17s vs 32s). EP helps precisely where the workload is cache-fed and compute can be spread.
build
Engine build is a live constraint. Benchmarked on a model-specific vLLM 0.19.1rc1 image. The stock vLLM 0.23.0 image fails to boot MiniMax-M2 on a missing tokenizer backend (sentencepiece/tiktoken) — a packaging gap, one-line fixable, but a hard blocker out-of-the-box.

Methodology & caveats

  • Hardware is B200 (SM100), not a prior-generation card. Absolute throughput reflects Blackwell's higher HBM bandwidth and FP8 throughput — treat numbers as B200 figures; the relative config deltas (caching, offload, parallelism) are the transferable findings.
  • Shared-prefix scenarios use a synthetic, byte-identical prefix. Measured hit-rate (90% / 99.6%) is an upper bound on what variable real traffic would achieve; aggregate throughput on cached scenarios is correspondingly optimistic.
  • Partial sweep. Run covered TP4 and TP4+EP4 × {GPU-only, CPU-offload} × 3 scenarios (59 points). NVMe KV-tiering and additional DP parallelism shapes were not completed in this window.
  • Cache-hit is read from server-side Prometheus counters, not the per-request cached_tokens field (unset on this build) — client-side cache measurement would read 0 and be misleading.
  • Build: vLLM 0.19.1rc1 (model-specific image), --moe-backend triton (avoids the FlashInfer FP8-MoE float32-router-logits assertion on SM100). Stock 0.23.0 needs a tokenizer-backend dependency added before it will boot this model.
  • Cold-start on B200 is ~35–40 min (weight load alone ~25 min for 214 GB FP8) — size readiness probes accordingly.
