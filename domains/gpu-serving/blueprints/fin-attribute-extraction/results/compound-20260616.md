## Benchmark signal
This was a pure benchmark session across three GPU architectures (g7e/sm_120, H200/sm_90, B200/sm_100) on the qwen3-next-bench EKS cluster in us-east-2. No deployment or audit cycle — lessons captured directly from benchmark execution and bottleneck analysis. Key findings: (1) full CUDA-graph capture is sm-arch-specific and is the ~2× differentiator; (2) per-replica SLO-safe knee g7e ~12, H200 ~20, B200 ~22 RPS; (3) mnbt ≥ ISL wins ~20% for prefill-dominated launch-bound workloads.

## Notes
- L1 (CUDA-graph arch-specificity) is a critical pattern that refutes the "launch-bound → bigger GPU won't help" intuition. The launch bound was engine/graph-capture-specific, not hardware-fundamental.
- L2 (missing node SG) caused a multi-hour failure cascade on the first B200 encounter — elevated to prevent recurrence on other clusters with similar nodegroup launch patterns.
- L5 and L6 (mnbt sizing, MoE tile tuning) are the two software levers validated during tuning follow-up. Both generalizable beyond this model.
- L7 (nvidia-smi dmon vs DCGM PROF) is a methodological lesson — DCGM point-samples missed the bursty peaks, continuous dmon gave the reliable read.
