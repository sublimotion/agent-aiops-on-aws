NVFP4 ≠ free win. Quality-gate PASSED (fixes bugs, tool-calls work; not a fallback — same trtllm runner). Wins only single-stream (+15% c1, less weight BW) and VRAM (56 vs 94 GB/GPU). 3. Long-context — prefill-compute-bound (16k vs 31k)

Regime: prefill-compute-bound, NOT KV-capacity-bound [gauge-inferred — DCGM PROF unavailable on driver-580]. Throughput plateaus while TTFT explodes; 0 errors/OOM to c=256. SLO-safe (15s TTFT) to ~c64 (16k) / ~c32 (31k). MNBT=8192 best at the knee (5.5s vs 7.9s default @ c16). B300 would NOT relieve the knee (its edge is KV capacity; this workload is compute-bound) — B300 only worth it to unlock the TP4+DP2 layout that 753GB FP8 can't fit on 180GB B200.
